## Implementation Constraints

- Do not use mock fallback when backend/session join fails.
- Do not submit orders in this batch. Order submit belongs to Batch 3.
- Do not remove mock files from the repository; only stop primary cart/session
  UI from depending on them.
- Cart source of truth is backend `CartSnapshot`.
- All cart mutations must pass the latest `expectedCartVersion`.
- On conflict, rollback optimistic UI and refetch cart. Do not force overwrite.
- Keep Step 2.2 visual behavior where practical: cart drawer, quantity controls,
  note editing, cart pill.
