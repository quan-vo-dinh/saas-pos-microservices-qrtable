## Implementation Constraints

- QR URL exposes tenant slug, not internal tenant id.
- Do not add subdomain routing in this batch.
- Do not upload QR images to Cloudinary.
- Do not add bulk PDF export.
- Do not add a PWA camera scanner.
- Do not add new permissions. Use `CATALOG_GET_LIST` for
  `GET /admin/tenant/current`.
- `GET /public/tenants/:slug` must not require staff auth.
- QR token must be stored opaque token and regenerated token must differ.
- Regenerating QR invalidates only future scans. It must not close active
  sessions.
- PWA may keep `VITE_TENANT_ID` only for legacy dev URLs without `tenant`.

Manual demo checklist:

1. Management App table QR dialog displays a real QR.
2. QR URL contains `tenant`, `table`, and `token`.
3. Opening/scanning QR starts Customer PWA tenant resolution.
4. PWA validates QR and joins session.
5. A second browser/device joins the same active session for occupied table.
6. Regenerate QR changes token.
7. Old QR URL fails validation for future scans.
8. New QR URL works.
