## Implementation Constraints

- Do not send item arrays to `POST /customer/orders`.
- Do not create offline order queue or mock fallback.
- Do not implement cash confirmation. Step 2.4 ends at bill `PENDING_PAYMENT`;
  real payment is Phase 3.
- Socket.IO `/orders` events are not source of truth. Invalidate/refetch REST
  queries instead of mutating local order/cart state directly.
- Ignore non-matching tenant/session/order events.
- Preserve Step 2.2 success animation and route to the real `order.id`.
