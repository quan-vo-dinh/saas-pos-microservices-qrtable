## Implementation Constraints

- Staff endpoints must use `@Authorization({ secured: true })` and
  `@Permissions(...)`.
- Effective protected chain should remain
  `UserGuard -> TenantGuard -> PermissionGuard`; do not start a global guard
  refactor unless tests prove it is required.
- Customer endpoints should rely on session/tenant context and Order Service
  ownership checks.
- BFF emits WebSocket events directly only after successful TCP responses.
- Do not consume Kafka from BFF in Step 2.4.
- Keep route and permission mapping aligned with
  `docs/architecture/permission-matrix.md`.
